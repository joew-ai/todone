## Why

After labels establish lightweight organization, families can reduce repeated Todo entry with daily routines. The core need is predictable current work without yesterday's missed work pressuring children to mark it complete merely to clear the list.

## What Changes

- Add daily recurrence templates that create dated Todo occurrences in the household timezone.
- Keep previous rounds out of active lists and record their terminal completed, missed, or skipped outcomes.
- Allow parents to archive a current occurrence as skipped and preserve read-only history for children.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `daily-recurring-rounds`: Daily routine templates, occurrence rollover, and non-intrusive historical rounds.

### Modified Capabilities

- `todo-lifecycle-history`: Adds recurrence-specific terminal outcomes to history.

## Impact

- Post-MVP; depends on date-only Todos, history, and labels.
- Adds scheduled or lazy idempotent occurrence generation and household-timezone logic.
- Excludes weekly/monthly schedules, ordered routine checklists, notifications, points, and rewards.
