## Why

Once durable routine history exists, parents need a calm way to notice patterns without turning family tasks into rankings or punishment. A focused summary can support conversations and adjustments to routines.

## What Changes

- Add parent-only routine outcome summaries by child and recurrence template.
- Surface completed, missed, and skipped counts for selectable historical periods.
- Preserve child privacy by excluding sibling comparisons and child-facing rankings.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `routine-trends`: Parent-only, non-gamified summaries of routine outcomes.

### Modified Capabilities

- `daily-recurring-rounds`: Adds summarized access to historical recurrence outcomes.
- `weekly-monthly-recurring-rounds`: Includes supported weekly and monthly outcomes in summaries.

## Impact

- Post-MVP; depends on daily recurrence and optionally incorporates later weekly/monthly recurrence.
- Adds read-only aggregation/query behavior and parent views.
- Excludes points, streaks, leaderboards, rankings, automated consequences, and notification campaigns.
